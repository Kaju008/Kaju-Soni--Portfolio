I've always been drawn to the kind of visuals that stop a scroll, pull you into a moment, and for a few seconds turn the screen into another world. That's what I try to put into everything I make.

Eight-plus years in, my work spans end-to-end video production, visual storytelling, motion graphics and editing, across YouTube long-form and Shorts, social, brand, marketing and advertising creative.

Over time, the role has grown from making the work to leading it: guiding teams, shaping ideas, solving production problems, and carrying a concept from brief to final frame, at a pace of 60+ videos in a single month.

AI is the newest chapter. I experiment with generative image and video tools to widen what a creative team can imagine and make, with craft and storytelling always at the centre.

Outside work, I'm a serious food enthusiast and happiest among forests, trails and mountains. I'll happily trade a busy weekend for an unfamiliar road, a great meal, or a sunrise worth waking up for.

Still curious. Still creating. Always chasing a better way to tell the next story.
